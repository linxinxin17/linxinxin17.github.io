CASA GILLARDI

2025 / 3D virtual environment, digital scene design

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - 3D modeling and scene composition
  • - Interior atmosphere, lighting, and object styling
  • - Virtual architectural visualization
  • - Valencia, Spain

Casa Gillardi is a digital 3D environment project developed through virtual space construction, material definition, and atmospheric lighting design.

The work focuses on the translation of architectural references into an inhabitable visual scene, combining interior composition, color, objects, vegetation, and lighting to construct a coherent spatial mood.

Through detailed renderings of domestic spaces and decorative elements, the project explores how digital environments can evoke physical presence, memory, and spatial experience.
