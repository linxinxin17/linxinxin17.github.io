VEIL OF LIGHT

2025 / 2.5D puzzle-platform game, four-person team project

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - Unity, 2D illustration, scene modelling and lighting
  • - Chinese shadow-puppet visual language and narrative game design
  • - Xin Lin: concept, art direction, character art, technical pipeline, camera and lighting
  • - Team project

Veil of Light is a narrative 2.5D puzzle-platform game shaped by the visual language of Chinese shadow puppetry. The player guides a puppet-like protagonist through temple ruins, forests and a city, using light and shadow to uncover a path forward.

My contribution established the initial concept and visual direction, including character art, the full technical art pipeline, camera and lighting, and selected scene models. The project was developed collaboratively by a four-person team.

Character Design and Production

Protagonist · Yi

Yi is conceived as an ancient archer. His bow and arrows, traditional helmet and distinctive boots reinforce his warrior identity while supporting the story and movement language of the game.

Cultural reference

The protagonist draws on the Chinese legend of Hou Yi. The archer motif turns a familiar cultural image into the central visual and narrative identity of the game.

Design language

Strong contours, flat silhouettes and overlapping layers reference shadow-puppet construction. Segmented limbs preserve the lightweight, articulated feeling of a puppet within the 2.5D stage.

Production method

The character was modelled in Blender, imported into Unity at a scale of 1.6 metres, then rigged so the limbs swing naturally without losing the graphic silhouette.

Five-element palette

Red, black and golden yellow form the dominant palette, communicating courage, strength and combat energy. Cream and green provide balance and connect the design to the five-element colour system.

  • Metal
  • Wood
  • Water
  • Fire
  • Earth
