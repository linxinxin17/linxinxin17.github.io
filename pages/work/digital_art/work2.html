CHINESE-STYLE PAINTED WORLD

2025 / 3D scene modelling and virtual environment

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - Blender modelling, texturing and rendering
  • - Blue-green landscape painting as a spatial reference
  • - S-shaped narrative path and immersive scene composition

Chinese-style Painted World translates the blue-green landscape tradition into an immersive 3D environment. Taking A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains as a visual point of departure, the project turns painted mountains, water and architecture into a world that can be entered and explored.

An S-shaped route guides the viewer through changing spatial layers, balancing the atmosphere of a scroll painting with the scale and rhythm of a virtual landscape.
