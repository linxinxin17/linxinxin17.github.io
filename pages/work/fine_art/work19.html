ARCHITECTURAL MODEL

2022 / Mixed-media architectural model / 28 x 31.7 x 30 cm

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - Mixed-media model making
  • - Hand-built structure, surface and roof details
  • - Spatial observation and small-scale reconstruction

This architectural model reconstructs a traditional Chinese courtyard dwelling at small scale. It translates the building's tiled roofs, enclosed courtyard, doors, windows and everyday domestic details into a compact spatial study.

The reference photograph and completed model are presented together to show the movement from observation to material interpretation. The work was completed in Valencia, Spain, in 2022.
