CHINESE PAINTING: SONG AND YUAN DYNASTY LANDSCAPE COPIES

2024 / Chinese ink-and-colour painting studies

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - Ink and colour on paper
  • - Copying, brushwork and compositional study
  • - References from Song and Yuan dynasty landscape painting

This group of studies develops an understanding of traditional Chinese landscape painting through close copying. Each original is followed by my painted copy, allowing brush rhythm, spatial construction and tonal structure to be read as a visual dialogue.

The references include Wang Meng's Dwelling in the Qingbian Mountains, Wang Shen's Boating on the Lotus Pond, and Guo Xi's Waiting for the Ferry.
