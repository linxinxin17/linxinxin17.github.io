BREATH BETWEEN US

2026 / Master's graduation project, interactive soft sculpture

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - EEG and GSR sensing, flexible pneumatic structure
  • - Arduino IDE, Node.js, HTML/CSS/JavaScript and Onshape
  • - Projection, light, sound and scent
  • - Sichiray EEG 2.0, Grove GSR and Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6

Developed as my master's graduation project, Breath Between Us is an interactive soft sculpture that gives form to the often-invisible interval between bodily sensing and emotional presence. EEG and GSR signals activate a flexible pneumatic body, while projection, light, sound and scent create an enveloping sensory environment.

The work invites visitors to approach, pause and recognise how a shared space can respond to subtle changes in attention and affect. The installation was presented at Raiosha Gallery, Keio University, Yokohama, Japan.
