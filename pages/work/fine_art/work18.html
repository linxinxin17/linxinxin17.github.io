3D PRINTING

2025 / PLA prototypes and fabrication studies

  • TECHNIQUES:
  • - PLA 3D printing and physical prototyping
  • - Iterative object design and fabrication tests
  • - Tactile form, modular joints and functional scale

This page brings together two PLA studies made in 2025: the conceptual Braille Touch Bracelet prototype and a 3D printing practice object. Both works explore how digital forms become tactile, usable objects through repeated fabrication tests.

The bracelet investigates modular links, flexible movement and tactile sequence, while the practice piece tests contour, scale and surface finish. Together they document 3D printing as both a prototyping method and a way of thinking through form.
